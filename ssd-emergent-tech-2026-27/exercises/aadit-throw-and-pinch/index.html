Throw & Pinch

camera clay, no kiln required

How to sculpt

  • 📷Enable your camera and it becomes the backdrop — the clay sits right in the scene with you.
  • ☝️Index finger alone — precise push.
  • ✌️Two-finger press (index+middle) — push.
  • 🤏Pinch thumb + index — pull; the gap between your fingers sets the size.
  • ✋Open hand/palm — flatten the clay underneath it.
  • 🖐️Three fingers (index+middle+ring), either hand — rotate / manipulate: sideways spins the clay, up/down tilts it.
  • 👍Thumb horizontal (pointing sideways) — move the clay on the x/y axis.
  • 👍Thumb vertical (pointing up/down) — move the clay on the z axis.
  • 🤲Both hands pinching — pull apart or together to scale, and swing them around each other to rotate.
  • ⇋Mirror sculpts both sides of the clay at once, symmetrically.
  • 🎨The Color swatch changes the clay's color.
  • 🎚️The Detail stepper changes how fine the clay mesh is — no upper limit, though very high levels get slow. Your sculpt carries over to the new resolution.
  • ⬇Download STL saves your sculpt as a 3D-printable file.
  • 🖱️No camera? Click-drag to pull, hold Shift to push, hold Ctrl/⌘ to flatten. Right-drag orbits the camera, scroll to zoom.
